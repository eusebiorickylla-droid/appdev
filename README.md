## 🧠 Plot Summary
**W – Two Worlds Apart is a South Korean series about Oh Yeon-joo, a cardiothoracic surgeon who gets pulled into the world of a popular webtoon, W, created by her father. She meets the webtoon's protagonist, Kang Chul, a wealthy and successful businessman who was once an Olympic gold medalist, and their lives become intertwined as they navigate the blurry lines between their two worlds.**
